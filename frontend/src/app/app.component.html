A NOTE FROM THE ARCHIVIST

About me.

Computer science student, systems-minded builder, and lifelong learner.

I enjoy building systems from the ground up and understanding how they work beneath the surface. I’m especially drawn to systems engineering, computer architecture, Linux, and the ideas that connect different areas of computer science. I like learning across domains and technologies, then turning that curiosity into practical software and hardware experiments.

I’m open to work across software domains, except frontend development.

AREAS I LIKE TO EXPLORE

My projects move between software, hardware, and the systems that connect them.

01   /   SYSTEMS ENGINEERING

Systems engineering

Linux, operating systems, filesystems, bootloaders, compilers, and hypervisors. I enjoy designing and understanding systems close to the machine with Rust, C, C++, and x86 assembly.

02   /   BACKEND ENGINEERING

Backend systems

APIs, services, databases, and the infrastructure behind applications. I’m interested in building dependable backend software and understanding how its components communicate, scale, and handle data.

03   /   NETWORKS

Protocols & infrastructure

From IP, ICMP, and DNS to web services, distributed tools, and storage. I’m interested in how reliable systems communicate and work together.

04   /   INTELLIGENCE

AI, ML & language

Search and NLP, machine learning, LLMs, retrieval augmented generation, model adaptation, and reinforcement learning. I’m curious about making tool-using AI systems more capable and dependable.

05   /   ROBOTICS

Embedded & physical systems

Rover navigation, sensors, Gazebo simulation, firmware, and custom electronics. I like seeing software meet physical constraints through devices such as ESP boards and Raspberry Pi.

06   /   FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY

Software & finance

Banking technology, payments, capital markets, and the databases behind them. I’m interested in the engineering challenges where financial systems and software meet.
